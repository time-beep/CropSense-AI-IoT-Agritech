2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================

What is happening right now:
- Fungal or bacterial infection starts inside the leaf at cellular level.
- Visible yellowing or spots appear only after 7 to 14 days of internal damage.
- By the time a farmer sees it, 40 to 60% of that plant is already damaged.
- Calling an agronomist costs Rs.500 to Rs.2000 per visit and takes days.
- Lab testing: send sample, wait 1 to 2 weeks, get generic result.

Scale of the Problem:
----------------------------------------------------------
Statistic                                  | Value
----------------------------------------------------------
Crop loss due to disease (India/year)      | ~Rs.90,000 crore
Farmers with access to agronomist          | Less than 15%
Average response time after symptom        | 3 to 7 days (too late)
Small / marginal farmers in India          | 146 million
----------------------------------------------------------

Why it is rarely addressed:
Existing solutions like drone imaging and satellite NDVI are expensive
(Rs.5 to 15 lakh), need internet, and give field-level data — not
plant-level early warning.

5. AI / ML COMPONENT
================================================================

Model Specifications:
----------------------------------------------------------
Model Type      : Decision Tree / Small Neural Network
Framework       : TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers
Runs On         : ESP32 — no internet required
Input Features  : NIR ratio, RED ratio, Temperature, Humidity, Time-of-day
Output Classes  : Healthy / Fungal Stress / Bacterial Stress / Nutrient Deficiency
----------------------------------------------------------

NDVI Formula Used:
NDVI = (NIR - RED) / (NIR + RED)

Healthy plant   -->  NDVI: 0.6 to 0.9
Early stress    -->  NDVI: 0.3 to 0.6   <-- CropSense detects HERE
Visible damage  -->  NDVI: below 0.3    <-- Traditional farming detects here

How Optical Sensing Works:
    NIR LED (850nm)
        | light
   [  L E A F  ]
        | transmitted light
    Photodiode
        |
    ADS1115 (ADC)
        |
    ESP32 reads voltage ratio

Healthy leaf  --> absorbs specific NIR % --> known ratio
Diseased leaf --> cell structure changes  --> ratio shifts

Training Pipeline:
Step 1: Collect Dataset
        -> PlantVillage dataset (54,000+ labeled leaf images)
        -> Convert to spectral signature equivalents

Step 2: Feature Extraction
        -> Extract NDVI, chlorophyll index, water content index

Step 3: Train Model (Python + sklearn / TensorFlow)
        -> Keep model under 50KB to fit in ESP32 flash

Step 4: Convert to TFLite
        -> tflite_convert -> .tflite model file

Step 5: Deploy to ESP32
        -> Flash model using Arduino IDE / PlatformIO

Model Performance Targets:
----------------------------------------------------------
Metric                    | Target
----------------------------------------------------------
Accuracy                  | > 85%
False Positive Rate       | < 10%
Inference Time on ESP32   | < 200 ms
Model Size                | < 45 KB
